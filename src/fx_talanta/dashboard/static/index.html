    Session-aware rules

    Choose the trading session and the range used for breakout research. Apply these settings to the JSON, then review the mapping again. Existing results are unchanged.

    Tokyo 09–18 local; London and New York 08–17 local, with daylight-saving handling. The UTC window remains an additional cap. Session ranges use all complete reference bars, not signal_period. Missing or incomplete ranges block signals. These are research conventions, not holiday calendars.

    Supported: H1 EMA trend with M15 breakout or EMA pullback, simple ATR stop, reward/risk target, time exit and UTC weekday session. Calendar veto uses historical market health; arbitrary news rules and combined-pair portfolio backtests are not implemented.

    One pair per study. Select the pair matching the strategy you intend to test.

    Windows and cost assumptions are fixed for each dataset. Registration caps risk at the lower of the dataset policy and your frozen candidate limits.
